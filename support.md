Baseline Guard for Jira customer support is available through the public
[Baseline Guard support portal](https://github.com/levybd/baseline-guard-legal/issues).

## Open a support ticket

Choose **New issue** in the support portal, then select the template that best
matches your request:

- **Bug report:** Baseline Guard is not behaving as expected or displays an
  error.
- **Support request:** You need help with installation, access, licensing,
  configuration, or using the app.
- **Feature request:** You want to suggest an improvement or new capability.

Provide only the minimum non-sensitive information needed to understand the
request. Helpful details can include the Baseline Guard version, the
approximate time of the problem, the selected reporting period and audience,
the visible error code, and non-sensitive reproduction steps.

Do **not** post secrets, passwords, access tokens, credentials, personal data,
confidential Jira content, report bodies, customer information, or security
vulnerabilities in a public GitHub issue.

## Private account, privacy, and security reports

For account, billing, privacy, or security matters, email
**help@levyautomations.com** instead of opening a public issue. Security
vulnerabilities must be reported privately to that address and must not be
disclosed through the public support portal.

In an initial private report, avoid sending credentials, access tokens, or
unnecessary Jira content. We may request additional non-sensitive information
after reviewing the report.

## Support hours and response target

Support hours are Monday–Friday, 9:00 AM–5:00 PM Pacific Time, excluding U.S.
federal holidays.

Our target is to provide an initial response within one business day. This is
an operating target, not a guaranteed response or resolution time and not a
contractual service-level agreement.
